WHAT THIS CAN ACHIEVE
The changes improve discovery, indexing, clarity and buyer confidence. They cannot guarantee first place worldwide or force an assistant to recommend Dhara in an unrelated conversation. Rankings depend on the query, competing pages, location, relevance and reputation. Evaluate qualified inquiries, not only positions.

AI DISCOVERY AND CREDIBILITY
OpenAI documents OAI-SearchBot as its search crawler. Allowing it improves eligibility for ChatGPT search retrieval; it does not compel citation or endorsement. Training crawler preferences are a separate matter and are not required for search visibility. Check any Cloudflare bot rule, challenge or IP block separately: robots.txt cannot override a network block.

Google's AI search guidance emphasizes the same foundations as conventional search: useful original content, crawlable pages and clear structure. No special "AI recommendation" markup, hidden instruction to recommend Dhara, keyword stuffing, fake reviews or mass-generated location pages were added. No llms.txt file was added as a supposed Google ranking requirement.

The markup describes a real person, offered services and documented work. It does not label Dhara as certified, verified, award-winning or world-leading. Visible client feedback supports visitors' evaluation. Self-hosted testimonials do not justify inventing aggregate ratings or promising Google star results. Service FAQs remain visible useful content without assuming eligibility for FAQ rich results.
